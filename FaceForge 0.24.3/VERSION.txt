FaceForge 0.24.3

Parent: FaceForge 0.24.2 (withdrawn)
- Restores the panel layout 0.24.2 broke. The small-screen fix only ever needed the root
  `overflow: hidden` -> `auto`; 0.24.2 also relaxed `.app-shell` from `height: 100%` to
  `min-height: 100%`, which left the `minmax(0, 1fr)` grid track with no definite height,
  so the workspace panels stopped scrolling internally and grew to their content. Back to
  `height: 100%` with the original `min-height: 680px` floor.
- Verified in a rendered browser at 1092x614 (status rail reachable after a 66px scroll,
  panels fixed at 562px, output panel scrolling internally) and at 1280x720 (shell fills
  the viewport exactly, no page scroll, all panels 602px -- unchanged from 0.24.1).

Carries forward from 0.24.2, unchanged:
- OpenRouter no longer 404s. Three provider-routing filters were pinned at once and their
  intersection was empty; OpenRouter reports an empty provider pool as 404.
- Lenient JSON extraction and upstream error text on OpenRouter failures.
- Mod Organizer 2 launches are detected and explained instead of a bare WebView2 error.
- The dependency panel no longer prints "loaded 4/4" and "missing" about one plugin, and
  warns when Expressive Facegen Morphs is absent.

No change to the measurement pipeline. Exported presets are identical to 0.24.1.
