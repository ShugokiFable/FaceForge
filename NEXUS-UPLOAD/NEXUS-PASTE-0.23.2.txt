CHANGELOG — paste into the Nexus "Changelog" box
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

0.23.2 - Hotfix: restores sliders that 0.23.0 wrongly removed

- Fixed: on High Poly Head setups, 0.23.0 and 0.23.1 silently dropped about 42
  face sliders from every exported preset. Faces came out coarser and more
  exaggerated than 0.22.0. Slider output is back to the full set.
- Cause: FaceForge decides which sliders your head can actually move by reading
  your installed morph files. It only read loose files, so it never saw that
  High Poly Head registers its own EFM/CME/ECE/RANs morph set from a config
  packed inside High Poly Head.bsa - and concluded those sliders were dead.
- FaceForge now detects archives whose morph registrations it cannot read and
  refuses to remove anything while any are present. It can confirm a slider
  works; it will no longer guess that one does not.
- Also fixed a config parser bug: a single registration line can list several
  morph files, and only the first was being read.

If you exported a preset with 0.23.0 or 0.23.1, re-export it with 0.23.2.
