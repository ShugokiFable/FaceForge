# FaceForge 0.24.1

Hotfix on accurate brows and honest UI.

## Fixes
- **Brow baselines measured** — brow width/thickness/height use rendered calibration (were 30–63% low guesses); full authority again
- **Forehead occlusion** — fringe/hair covering the brow fades those axes instead of treating hair as brow geometry
- **UI honesty** — removed false HPH calibration line; confidence fade is no longer described as “left at neutral”
- Still includes **0.24.0**: one measurement per slider family; iris/lip-gap stay estimated and capped
