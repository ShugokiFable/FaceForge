FACEFORGE 0.23.1

TO OPEN:
Double-click "FaceForge 0.23.1 - STANDALONE.exe".

Use Quick Photo for one front image, or Guided Multi-View for front/left/right
photos or a slow turn video.

TO SHARE:
Send that one EXE. The recipient does not need the source folder or a ZIP.

Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime is required and is normally already installed
on supported Windows 10 and Windows 11 systems.

Optional ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini subscription vision requires that provider's
official CLI. FaceForge Settings opens the official install/sign-in flow.
